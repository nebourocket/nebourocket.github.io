Research

Two topics, a long way apart.

Now, master's program

How people and dogs interact

I read videos of people and dogs closely, frame by frame, to see what each one does and what changes over time. The order I work in:

  1. Watch the videos closely.
  2. Label behavior frame by frame in ELAN.
  3. Cluster the labels into recurring states.
  4. Trace how one state turns into the next.

Step 2 finished on 13 Nov 2026. Clustering is next.

Before, undergraduate

A magnetic nozzle for a laser-fusion rocket

A laser-fusion rocket makes very hot plasma. A magnetic field can push it out the back without the plasma touching the walls. I tested whether magnetohydrodynamic (MHD) acceleration works for this, by simulation: CFD software for the flow, Python (NumPy, Matplotlib) for the analysis and plots.

Shown twice: a poster at a national space conference (Nov 2024) and an English talk at an international propulsion conference (Mar 2025).
