Research

Two topics, a long way apart.

Now: master's thesis, JAIST, Social Signal Processing and Multimodal Interaction Lab (supervisor: Prof. Shogo Okada)

How do a person's instructions and actions relate to whether a dog solves a detour task?

Most work on estimating a dog's state watches only the dog. The voice, gaze and movement of the person next to it are rarely measured. So I built a setup that measures the dog and the person at the same time.

With Purdue University's College of Veterinary Medicine, we ran a V-shaped fence detour task: 30 dogs and 40 people meeting them for the first time, 64 sessions, 192 trials. Trial 1 the dog tries alone; before trials 2 and 3 the person shows the way. The project is called CRONOS.

Of the 36 sessions where the dog failed trial 1, 13 (36%) succeeded after the demonstration. Dogs that succeeded tended to move away from the person; distance and position features remain as exploratory candidates.

  1. Measure. Wearable sensors on dog and person (IMU, ECG, skin conductance), three room cameras and four microphone channels, all synchronized.
  2. Extract. About 170,000 features per trial from 9 streams: dog 2D and 3D pose, IMU, ECG, human 3D pose, physiology, audio, cross-modal coupling, and the interaction timeline. ID swaps in human pose tracking fixed by hand; behavior annotated in ELAN.
  3. Compare. Successful trials are short, so trial length is controlled for: Mann–Whitney U, FDR, Cliff's delta, partial rank correlation on length-residualized values, permutation tests.
  4. Model. K-Means state sequences, temporal-order rules, hidden Markov models, and a machine-learning classifier for success.

Python, Docker, ViTPose and RTMW3D pose estimation, ELAN, statistical testing, a GPU server with Slurm, LaTeX that pulls every number in the thesis and slides from the data.

Before: undergraduate, Kanazawa Institute of Technology, Moriai Lab

MHD analysis of acceleration and control by a magnetic nozzle in a laser-fusion rocket engine

A laser-fusion rocket makes very hot plasma. A magnetic nozzle can push it out the back without the plasma touching the walls. I tested by simulation whether magnetohydrodynamic (MHD) acceleration works for this: CFD software for the flow, Python (NumPy, Matplotlib) for the analysis and plots.

  • Nov 2024. 68th Space Sciences and Technology Conference, poster: “A study on numerical analysis methods for plasma acceleration and control by magnetic nozzles”.
  • Mar 2025. AJCPP 2025, oral, in English: “Numerical analysis method for plasma acceleration and control by magnetic nozzles”.
