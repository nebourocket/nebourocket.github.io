MODULE 01 · HABITAT NODE

About

Profile / Mission Log

I'm a master's student researching human–dog interaction analysis. My work focuses on annotating videos of dogs and people interacting, and analyzing them with clustering and state-transition analysis.

I'm also writing a Kindle book for undergraduates who are on the fence about graduate school — the first chapter is done. On note, I publish paper-reading and tech explainer articles, everyday essays, and a morning “Daily News” series.

I like a minimalist life with fewer things, and quiet days that include strength training. Living slowly and simply matters to me — even while my research lives among stars, models, and data.

Mission: Make the invisible patterns between a person and a dog visible — with careful annotation, honest analysis, and words anyone can follow.

My Apps — free to use, no sign-up

Two web apps I built and run myself. Both open in the browser, work on a phone like an app, and keep your records only on your own device.

APP / 01Pro ¥500 one-time

PFC Calculator — PFCかんたん計算ツール

Pick a food, enter the grams, and today's calories and PFC (protein, fat, carbs) are totaled instantly, with progress bars against your daily goals. Records stay in your browser's localStorage only — nothing is sent to a server.

  • All the data: all 2,537 foods from Japan's Standard Tables of Food Composition (2023 supplement), plus 29,283 packaged products from Open Food Facts — searchable in everyday words.
  • Free basics: calorie & PFC totals and goal bars, weight log (7-day average, 30-day graph), training log with estimated 1RM and personal-best notices, recent-foods shortcuts, and backup export / import.
  • Three languages: Japanese, English and Simplified Chinese, switchable in one tap.

Pro — ¥500, one-time (no subscription): 30-day calorie graph with goal line; automatic goal calculation from sex, age, height, weight, activity and purpose; your personal required calories, reverse-calculated from your own intake and weight trend toward a target weight and deadline (with different targets for training and rest days); up to 200 custom foods (free: 5); reusable meal sets; CSV export; weekly sets per muscle group; and a 100-point daily score with streaks and best records. Payment is handled by Stripe — card details never reach the app — and Pro unlocks with a signed license key; the note membership code also works.

APP / 02FREE · OPEN DATA

Exercise Search — 筋トレ種目サーチ

Search 1,700+ exercises (1,781 in total) — barbell, dumbbell, bodyweight, cable, machine, kettlebell, stretching and more — by name, body part, equipment, or just by saying what you want to train, like “I want to train my back” or “abs at home.”

  • Body map: tap a muscle on the front / back figure to filter; each exercise shows its primary and secondary muscles on the figure.
  • Three languages: UI, names and instructions in English, Japanese and Simplified Chinese, with filters for muscle, equipment, type, level, force and mechanic.
  • Details & sharing: images, step-by-step instructions and detailed guides, shareable URLs, light / dark theme.
  • Logs straight into PFC Calculator: “Log in PFC Calculator” opens the PFC app with the exercise name and muscle group already filled in.

Built as a static site with vanilla JavaScript on open data from free-exercise-db and wger. Free, no sign-up, no tracking.
